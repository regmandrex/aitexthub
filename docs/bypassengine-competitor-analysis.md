**Source:** Semrush organic positions export, 2,312 keywords, snapshot ~2026-06-22.

## TL;DR

bypassengine.com is an **AI-humanizer / "bypass AI detection" / plagiarism-remover** site for students evading Turnitin, Canvas, GPTZero. Their traffic sits on three pillars:

1. **Humanizer/detector *review* pages** (brand-navigational: "grubby ai", "stealthwriter", "duey ai", "copyleaks") — **skip these.** Brand-nav intent, reputationally fraught, not our niche.
2. **"Can [LMS] detect…" informational cluster** (Canvas / Gradescope / Blackboard) — **their biggest pure-content win, KD 2–18, and we have ZERO pages.** This is the prime build target.
3. **Tool/utility keywords that overlap pages we already own** (watermark remover, sentence rewriter, text remover, code humanizer) — **strengthen, don't rebuild.**

Key constraint (see memory `indexing-status`): our domain is young (reg. Oct 2025), ~334 pages "crawled not indexed." Many ranking gaps are **authority/age**, not content. Prioritize accordingly: strengthening + internal-linking existing pages beats spinning up more thin pages that won't index.
